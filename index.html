How to use an ACBuy spreadsheet

An ACBuy spreadsheet is a directory for finding and comparing product listings. Open a CAFANS product page to find the original seller listing, then check whether your shopping agent accepts that source link.

Confirm the seller’s variant

Save the seller link, size, color and listing price together. Compare the seller’s garment measurements against a piece you own. For shoes, check whether the chart uses foot length or insole length.

Review warehouse QC photos

Match the size label and color to your order. If fit is uncertain, ask for a photo that shows both ends of the ruler. Check the agent’s exchange window before approving dispatch.

Compare shipping quotes

Use the same destination and packed box dimensions for each quote. Check the chargeable weight and ask whether handling, insurance and destination taxes are included.

Estimate your order cost

Use USD for each amount. The subtotal adds the figures you enter. Add taxes or duties under other fees when you have an estimate.

Common questions

Where do the product links go?

The two buttons at the top open the CAFANS homepage. Each product card opens its CAFANS detail page, where you can look for the original seller link.

Do the catalog prices include shipping?

The displayed figures are CAFANS item-price estimates. Check the seller’s current price and add domestic delivery, international shipping and any fees from your agent’s quote.

What if my size or color disappears?

Reopen the original seller listing and confirm the variant is still selectable. If it has disappeared, ask the agent to confirm availability before paying or compare another listing.
